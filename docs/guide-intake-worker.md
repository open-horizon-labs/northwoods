# Intake Worker Guide

**Your role:** You are responsible for scanning or photographing completed intake forms and uploading them to Northwoods for processing. The system automatically reads the form and pulls out the important information — you do not need to type anything in by hand. You can also download blank forms to hand out to clients.

---

## Signing In

1. Open your web browser and go to **https://northwoods.muness.com/**
2. You will see the TraverseLite sign-in page.
3. Enter your work email address and password.
4. Click **Sign in**.

After signing in you will land on the **Submit an intake** page. This is the only page you will use.

To sign out at any time, click the **Sign out** link in the top-right corner.

## Your Main Screen

The page has three sections stacked on top of each other:

1. **New submission** — the upload form where you submit scanned intake forms
2. **Blank forms** — a list of form types you can download to hand to clients
3. **My submissions** — a list of everything you have uploaded during this session, with a live status badge showing where each form is in the process

---

## Submitting an Intake Form

This is your most common task. Do this after a client has completed their form and you have scanned it or taken a photo.

**Step 1 — Choose the form type**

Under "New submission," find the **Form type** dropdown and select the type of form the client filled in (for example, "General Assistance Intake"). If no options appear, contact your administrator.

**Step 2 — Attach the scan or photo**

- On a **computer:** Click the file picker button, browse to the scan, and select it. The system accepts PDF files and image files (JPG, PNG).
- On a **phone or tablet:** You will see two buttons:
  - **Take photo** — opens your camera so you can photograph the form right away
  - **Upload file** — lets you choose an existing file from your device

You can only attach one file per submission. If a client's form is multiple pages, scan it as a single PDF before uploading.

**Step 3 — Submit**

Click the blue **Submit intake** button. The button will show "Submitting..." while the upload is in progress.

Once it goes through, you will see the form appear at the bottom of the page under "My submissions" with the status **Queued**.

**If you see an error message in red,** check that you selected a form type and attached a file, then try again. If the problem continues, contact your supervisor or system administrator.

---

## Understanding the Status Badges

After you submit, the status badge on your submission updates automatically. You do not need to refresh the page.

| Status           | What it means                                                                 |
|------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Queued**       | The file has been received and is waiting to be processed.                   |
| **Processing**   | The system is reading the form and pulling out the information.               |
| **Ready for Review** | Processing is done. A reviewer will check the extracted information.    |
| **Finalized**    | A reviewer has confirmed the information. The intake is complete.             |
| **Failed**       | Something went wrong during processing. Contact your supervisor.              |

Once a submission reaches "Ready for Review," your work is done for that form. The reviewer team takes it from there.

## Downloading Blank Forms

If a client needs a blank form to fill out, you can print one directly from Northwoods.

1. Scroll down to the **Blank forms** section.
2. Find the form type you need.
3. Click **Download blank** next to it.
4. A PDF will download to your computer. Open it and print it.

If a form type shows "No printable form available," contact your administrator to ask them to upload a blank PDF for that template.

## Common Questions

**Can I delete an upload I made by mistake?**
No. Once a form is submitted, only a reviewer or administrator can manage it. If you submitted the wrong file, let your supervisor know right away.

**My submissions list is empty when I log back in — where did my forms go?**
The "My submissions" list only shows forms uploaded during your current session. Once you sign out, the list clears. Your submissions are still in the system and will be reviewed — you have not lost them.

**I submitted a form but the status has been "Queued" for a long time. Is something wrong?**
Processing usually takes a minute or two. If it stays on Queued for more than 10 minutes, contact your supervisor.

**The file picker is not accepting my file.**
Make sure the file is a PDF or an image (JPG or PNG). Other file types are not accepted. If you have a TIFF or other format, convert it before uploading.
