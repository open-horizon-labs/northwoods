This is the operational access job implied by the assignment.

Success means users can find prior work by person, date, or template type and understand the state of a case without opening disconnected uploads one by one. The system becomes a working record, not just a processing pipeline.

Why it matters:
- It turns processed documents into usable operational knowledge.
- It supports continuity across multiple intake documents and review actions.
- It gives the RAG layer and review workflow a coherent case context to attach to.
