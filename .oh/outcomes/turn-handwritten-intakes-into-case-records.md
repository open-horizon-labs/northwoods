This is the core intake-system job implied by the assignment.

Success means an intake worker can choose a form template, upload a scanned document, and rely on the system to produce a structured draft record with visible extraction status, original-file access, and confidence metadata. The system should reduce manual re-entry while keeping enough transparency that staff can trust the result.

Why it matters:
- It establishes the main workflow foundation for everything else in the exercise.
- It turns document processing from storage-only into operational case intake.
- It creates the raw material needed for review, search, audit, and retrieval to be useful.
