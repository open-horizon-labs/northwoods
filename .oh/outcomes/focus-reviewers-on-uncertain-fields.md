This is the human-in-the-loop job implied by the assignment.

Success means reviewers do not need to re-process the whole document mentally. They can see what the machine extracted, understand where confidence is low, correct specific values, and finalize the document with an auditable history of what happened.

Why it matters:
- It is where trust in the system is either earned or lost.
- It makes confidence scoring operational instead of decorative.
- It converts OCR/AI output into a governed workflow rather than an opaque black box.
