This guards margin and delivery realism.

Why it matters:
- Interview exercises reward apparent completeness, but shallow breadth often hides weak judgment.
- A coherent upload → extract → review → finalize slice, with tenant safety, auditability, retrieval, and tests, says more about leadership than a long tail of half-finished features.
- This aligns with my personal aim to build and protect margin: preserve time and attention for the parts that prove trust, learning, and operational competence.

Implications for delivery:
- Prioritize the review workflow, tenancy proof, retrieval usefulness, and documentation before peripheral polish.
- Use synthetic data, simplified OCR, and bounded template design when they strengthen the end-to-end story.
- Reject 'while I am here' scope creep unless it directly reinforces the core workflow or the rubric's trust boundaries.

This guardrail helps the final system feel intentional rather than exhausted.
